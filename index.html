About BIRD Lab

Bioinspired intelligence · Robot design · Interdisciplinary innovation仿生智能 · 机器人设计 · 交叉创新

🧬 Learning from nature, creating for the future🧬 从自然中学习，为未来而创造

The Bioinspired Intelligent Robotic Design Lab (BIRD Lab) at Southern University of Science and Technology is dedicated to drawing inspiration from biological systems — not just mimicking their forms, but embedding their essential operational logic into the core of robotic systems.南方科技大学仿生智能机器人设计实验室（BIRD Lab）致力于从生物系统中汲取灵感——不只是模仿生物形态，而是将其本质运行逻辑嵌入机器人系统的核心。

We integrate mechanical engineering, control theory, artificial intelligence and materials science, focusing on suction adhesion mechanisms in robotics, biomimetic humanoid robotic systems, and soft robotics, providing innovative solutions for next-generation compliant, flexible, human-like humanoid robots, as well as healthcare, livelihood and underwater special operations.我们融合机械工程、控制理论、人工智能与材料科学，聚焦于机器人真空吸附机制、仿生人形机器人系统与软体机器人等前沿方向，为下一代柔顺、灵活、拟人的人形机器人，以及医疗、民生、水下特种作业等领域提供创新解决方案。
