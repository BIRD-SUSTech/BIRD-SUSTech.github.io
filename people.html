Tianqi Yue / 岳天奇

Principal Investigator · Associate Professor课题组组长 · 副教授

Hi, I'm an Associate Professor at the School of System Design and Intelligent Manufacturing, Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), and the Principal Investigator of the Bioinspired Intelligent Robotic Design Lab (BIRD Lab). Our philosophy is to draw inspiration from nature — not just by mimicking biological forms, but by capturing their essential operational logic and embedding that intelligence into the core of robotic systems, so that robots can interact with humans flexibly, safely, and naturally.大家好，我是南方科技大学自动化与智能制造学院的副教授、仿生智能机器人设计实验室（BIRD Lab）的课题组长。BIRD Lab 的理念是从自然中汲取灵感——不只是模仿生物的形态，而是抓住其本质运行逻辑，将这种智能嵌入机器人系统的核心，让机器人能够与人类灵活、安全、自然地交互。
