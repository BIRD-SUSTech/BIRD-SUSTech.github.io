🧑‍🎓 About Me个人简介

Hello, I am a PhD student focusing on soft robotics, with an emphasis on the design, fabrication, and application of novel artificial muscles. Previously involved in research on medical robots, now dedicated to exploring novel soft actuation technologies and advancing their applications in robotics and biomedical fields.你好，我是一名博士生，研究方向为软体机器人，重点研究新型人工肌肉的设计、制备与应用。此前曾参与医疗机器人研究，现致力于探索新型软体驱动技术，并推动其在机器人与生物医学领域的应用。
