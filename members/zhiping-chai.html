🧑‍🎓 About Me个人简介

I am a visiting scholar at SUSTech. By integrating physics-based simulation, data-driven modeling, and generative architectures, my research interest bridges the gap between computational intelligence and real-world physical systems.我是南方科技大学的访问学者。我的研究融合基于物理的仿真、数据驱动建模与生成式架构，致力于弥合计算智能与真实物理系统之间的鸿沟。

🔬 Research Interests研究方向
