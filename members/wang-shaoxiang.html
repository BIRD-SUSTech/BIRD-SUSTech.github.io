🧑‍🎓 About Me个人简介

Hi there, I am a visiting PhD student. My research focuses on soft robotics and nonlinear mechanical systems, exploring how nonlinear material, structural, and fluidic dynamics can be harnessed to create adaptive robotic behaviors with reduced reliance on centralized control.你好！我是一名访问博士生。我的研究聚焦于软体机器人与非线性机械系统，探索如何利用材料、结构及流体中的非线性动力学，构建对集中式控制依赖更低的适应性机器人行为。
