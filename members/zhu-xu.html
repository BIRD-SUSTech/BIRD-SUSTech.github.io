🧑‍🎓 About Me个人简介

Hi, I'm a PhD student in BIRD Lab, SUSTech. I earned dual bachelor's degrees in Computer Science and Automation from Zhejiang University followed by a Master's degree in Robotics from the University of Bristol. Then I worked for two years as a prediction algorithm engineer at Horizon Robotics (a leading autonomous driving/robotics company in China). With a strong interest in intelligent robotics, my current research focuses on the hardware design and intelligent control of bio-inspired humanoid robots.你好，我是 BIRD Lab 的博士生。我本科毕业于浙江大学（计算机科学与技术、自动化双学位），硕士毕业于布里斯托大学机器人学专业，之后在地平线（中国领先的自动驾驶/机器人公司）担任了两年预测算法工程师。我对智能机器人有浓厚兴趣，目前的研究聚焦于仿生人形机器人的硬件设计与智能控制。

🔬 Research Interests研究方向
